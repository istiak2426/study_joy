Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about visas, costs, and living in Japan.

Do I need to be fluent in Japanese to apply?
Not necessarily. While knowing Japanese is highly beneficial, many universities offer degree programs taught entirely in English. However, we recommend learning basic Japanese to make your daily life and part-time job search much easier.
What are the average tuition fees and living costs?
Tuition fees vary by institution, typically ranging from $5,000 to $15,000 USD per year. Living expenses, including rent, food, and transportation, usually average around $800 to $1,200 USD per month, depending on whether you live in a major city like Tokyo or a more regional area.
Am I allowed to work part-time on a student visa?
Yes! International students can work up to 28 hours per week during the academic semester and up to 40 hours per week during long school holidays. You will just need to apply for a "Permission to Engage in Activity Other Than That Permitted under the Status of Residence" at the airport or immigration office.
How does the student visa application process work?
Once you are accepted into a Japanese school, they will apply for a Certificate of Eligibility (COE) on your behalf through the Immigration Bureau in Japan. Once your COE is issued and sent to you, you can take it to your local Japanese embassy or consulate to officially apply for your student visa.
